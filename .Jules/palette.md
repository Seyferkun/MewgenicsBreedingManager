## 2023-10-27 - Enable QLineEdit Clear Buttons
**Learning:** Adding a native clear button (`setClearButtonEnabled(True)`) to `QLineEdit` search/filter fields significantly improves UX and keyboard accessibility by reducing the friction of clearing out entered text in frequently-cleared fields.
**Action:** When implementing PySide6 search or filter inputs, consistently enable the clear button alongside setting placeholder text to ensure a native, frictionless experience.
