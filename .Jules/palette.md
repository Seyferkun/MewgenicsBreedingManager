
## 2024-05-18 - PySide6 QLineEdit Clear Buttons for Search/Filters
**Learning:** For QLineEdits functioning as search or filter inputs in PySide6, users expect a native, single-click way to clear their query.
**Action:** Always enable `setClearButtonEnabled(True)` on `QLineEdit` components intended for filtering/searching to provide an integrated "X" button and consistent application behavior.
