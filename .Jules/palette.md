## 2024-05-18 - Enable QLineEdit Clear Buttons\n**Learning:** In desktop Qt/PySide applications, `QLineEdit` does not have a native clear button enabled by default, which can cause minor friction in search/filter forms.\n**Action:** Always enable `setClearButtonEnabled(True)` on `QLineEdit` widgets meant for search, filtering, or quick entry, providing a free native UX improvement without adding custom icons or logic.
