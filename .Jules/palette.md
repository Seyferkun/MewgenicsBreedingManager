## 2024-05-18 - Ensure native clear buttons for PySide6 line edits
**Learning:** PySide6 QLineEdits missing native clear buttons (`setClearButtonEnabled(True)`) and tooltips hurt search UX, especially in lists and filters.
**Action:** When working on PySide6 GUIs, ensure that search or filter inputs have `setClearButtonEnabled(True)` and helpful placeholders or tooltips set for accessibility and usability.
