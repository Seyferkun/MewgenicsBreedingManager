## 2026-10-07 - Native Clear Buttons on PySide6 Search Inputs
**Learning:** In PySide6, enabling native clear buttons on search fields `setClearButtonEnabled(True)` ensures consistent, accessible UX across views. Tooltips or placeholders may also be combined with this for better usability.
**Action:** Always enable the clear button on any search/filter `QLineEdit` to provide a simple, touch/mouse/keyboard accessible way to clear search input.
